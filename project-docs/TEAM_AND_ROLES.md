# Team and roles

Last updated: 20 August 2026

## Official hackathon team

The team has three members.

| Member | Current role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Basant Bhat | Guide and developer | Leads coding and technical implementation. Basant is not the registered team leader. |
| Gaurav | Research, innovation, and product thinking | Investigates problems, evidence, user needs, and solution ideas. Full name to be recorded. |
| Sachine | Research, innovation, and product thinking | Investigates problems, evidence, user needs, and solution ideas. Exact name spelling and full name must be confirmed. |

The registered team leader has not yet been recorded in this documentation. Do not guess it. Add the name after the registration details are confirmed.

Roles describe current responsibility, not exclusive ownership. Every important decision should name the people who researched, proposed, reviewed, coded, or approved it.

## Outside contributors

Pappu, Prashant, and other friends are not official team members. They may provide comments, research leads, criticism, or ideas.

Their contribution must not be presented as team work without attribution. If the project uses their text, code, design, data, image, or other material, record the item and written permission in `CONTRIBUTION_AND_RIGHTS_LOG.md` before using it in the submission.

## Working responsibilities

### Basant Bhat

- Convert approved product decisions into a working prototype.
- Use Codex meaningfully and record its contribution.
- Review generated code, tests, security, and limitations.
- Tell the research team when a proposed feature cannot be built or verified safely.

### Gaurav and Sachine

- Find real user problems and primary sources.
- Separate official rules from opinions and assumptions.
- Verify process rules, timelines, and user-facing guidance.
- Define the core journey and measurable before-and-after comparison.
- Record unresolved questions instead of filling gaps with assumptions.

### Whole team

- Approve the final problem statement and scope.
- Test the full journey.
- Review the demo, write-up, disclosures, and limitations.
- Confirm that no sensitive or unlicensed material is included.
