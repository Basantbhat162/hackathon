# Research workspace

Research in this folder is supporting material. It becomes durable project truth only when a verified claim, limitation, decision, or open question is promoted to the correct owner in `../project-docs/`.

## Folder map

- `workflows/`: research rules, staged prompts, raw Comet Browser and Perplexity results, and deferred collection plans. Start with `workflows/README.md`.
- `team/`: team-supplied ideas, interviews, project breakdowns, and working analysis.
- `external/`: outside articles, social-media material, and unverified research exports that have not been promoted.

## Placement rules

- Keep prompts and their returned results under the same workflow tree.
- Use dated result filenames when the workflow defines a naming convention.
- Keep team accounts separate from internet evidence; neither should be rewritten to fit the selected idea.
- Do not treat external material as an official hackathon rule or a product rule.
- Promote only verified conclusions to `../project-docs/RESEARCH_AND_DECISION_LOG.md`; link to the original material instead of copying it.
- Put temporary scraping, rendering, or transformation files in `../tmp/`, and reviewable generated deliverables in `../output/`.
