**Tool Kya Karega**

1. User apni problem batata hai simple bhasha mein: "Mera Income Tax refund March se atka hai"
2. AI usse **formal escalation notice** mein convert karta hai — sahi section cite karke (jaise CBDT ka refund timeline circular), sahi department address karke, deadline mention karke
3. Tool ek **countdown tracker** deta hai — statutory reply time (jaise 30 din) ke against
4. Agar deadline miss ho jaaye, tool automatically **next level escalation** generate karta hai (higher authority ko)
5. **"Escalation Ladder"** dikhta hai — Level 1 se Level 4 tak, kaunsa officer/authority agla step hai
