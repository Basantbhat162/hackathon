# Team lived-problem interview

Ask Basant, Gaurav, and Sachine separately. Record exact facts. Do not help the person reshape an answer to fit an idea.

## Questions for each member

1. Which Indian government or public-service website or digital service have you personally used?
2. What exact task were you trying to complete?
3. Did you complete it yourself, or directly help someone complete it from start to finish?
4. Where did the journey start and where was it supposed to end?
5. What exact step failed, confused you, rejected the request, or forced an offline visit?
6. What message, status, or explanation did the service show?
7. What did you try next?
8. How many attempts, visits, calls, documents, or days did it take? Use only what you remember or can verify.
9. Do you still have a safely redacted message, screenshot, receipt, email, or note that supports the account?
10. Did the problem involve another person's account or personal data? If yes, what direct role did you have and what permission do you have to discuss it?
11. What would a successful end-to-end journey have looked like?
12. Which part was a process or rule problem rather than only bad visual design?

## Record format

| Member | Service | Exact task | Direct role | Failure point | Evidence available | Sensitive data removed? | Journey start | Journey end | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `CONFIRMED`, `UNCLEAR`, or `NOT FIRST-HAND` in the status column.

Do not store unredacted personal evidence in this research folder.
