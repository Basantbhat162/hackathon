## Validation requirements

For each candidate:

- Identify the governing service, responsible authority, and exact jurisdiction.
- Locate the published user journey and the current official rules.
- Identify the exact failure point and the process, rule, state transition, handoff, or responsibility that causes or contributes to it.
- Separate a proven cause from a researcher inference.
- Find at least two independent sources for every material problem claim, including at least one Tier A or Tier B source where possible.
- Check whether the problem is current, historical, geographically limited, resolved, or unclear.
- Separate measured prevalence from individual anecdotes.
- Record contradictions and failed searches.
- Reject candidates supported only by copied articles, isolated social posts, SEO pages, inaccessible evidence, or unsupported claims.
- Do not infer that a team member faced the problem. Keep `TEAM CONFIRMATION REQUIRED` unless direct team evidence is pasted.
- Do not import rules from any other hackathon.

## Safety

- Do not visit transactional flows, log in, submit forms, enter data, use OTPs, test live services, automate or scrape government systems, or use undocumented APIs.
- Use only public non-transactional pages and published material.
- Do not reproduce personal or sensitive data.
