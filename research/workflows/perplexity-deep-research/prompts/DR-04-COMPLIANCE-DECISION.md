## Mandatory gate

Evaluate every candidate on all 11 checks:

1. The problem sits on an Indian public-service website or digital service, or the build is a new platform simplifying resident-to-administration interaction.
2. Someone on the official team personally faced or directly handled the problem.
3. One clearly defined user problem and one main journey from start to finish.
4. A working prototype, not design only. Every demonstrated feature works and no live integration is faked.
5. Codex is meaningfully involved in the build and its contribution can be explained.
6. Mock or synthetic sensitive data, no real Aadhaar, PAN, passwords, OTPs, payments, or health data, no testing or scraping live government systems, and no undocumented private APIs.
7. Fresh code, rights to every asset, and no old project with small changes.
8. The solution fixes a process, rule, state, responsibility, or backend problem rather than only the interface.
9. The team can deliver by 27 August 2026 a public demo, an ideally sub-three-minute video, and the required write-up. The repository is optional.
10. The experience is designed for mobile, slow connections, and limited digital experience.
11. The team can state plainly what is real, mocked, dependent, and limited.

Mark each check `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `UNCLEAR` and give exact evidence. Any `FAIL` means the idea fails as written. Any `UNCLEAR` means `HOLD`.

Do not assign a strategic score unless every mandatory check is `PASS`.

## Strategic score

Only for a gate-cleared candidate, score each factor 0, 1, or 2:

- AI necessity.
- Judge-visible demo moment under 60 seconds.
- Measurable improvement.
- Fit for three people and the time remaining.
- Differentiation.
- Correctness risk and availability of published rules.
- Evidence quality.
- Scope discipline.

Total out of 16:

- 12 to 16: `PASS - strong candidate`.
- 10 to 11: `PASS - worth developing`.
- 8 to 9: `PASS - but needs refinement` and name the required fix.
- Below 8: `REJECT - valid but strategically weak`.
- Any mandatory `FAIL`: `FAIL - does not meet hackathon requirements`.
- Any mandatory `UNCLEAR`: `HOLD - important unknowns`.
