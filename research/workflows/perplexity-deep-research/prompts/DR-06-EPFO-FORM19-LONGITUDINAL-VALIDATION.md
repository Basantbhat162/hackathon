## Locked topic

PF Nikal, narrowed: using synthetic or safely redacted input, help an EPFO member understand a blank or cryptic Form 19 final-settlement rejection, explain only what current official evidence supports, state when the cause cannot be determined, assemble an evidence checklist, prepare a mock EPFiGMS grievance, and track the mocked follow-up.

The proposed product must never promise to infer a rejection cause that EPFO did not provide, file a real grievance, connect to EPFO, or obtain settlement.

## Main research question

Is there enough current, correctly measured and source-backed evidence to justify building this exact Form 19 journey, and what product behavior can be supported safely by current official rules?

## Required analysis

### 1. Administrative scale

Find the strongest official statistics available for 2022-23 through 2025-26, with earlier years only when needed for a comparable trend.

Search EPFO, Ministry of Labour and Employment, Lok Sabha, Rajya Sabha, CAG, statutory or court material, official dashboards, annual reports, board records, grievance reports, and published annexures for:

- online and offline claims received;
- Form 19 claims received and settled;
- claims rejected or returned;
- claims rejected more than once;
- claims pending beyond 20 days;
- average or distribution of settlement time;
- auto-settled claims versus manually processed claims;
- EPFiGMS grievances by service category;
- grievances about withdrawal, settlement, rejection, delay, missing reasons, or repeated rejection;
- regional office variation when officially measured.

For every statistic identify the unit, claim types included, period, numerator, denominator, exact source table, and whether years are genuinely comparable.

Do not infer a rejection count by subtracting settled claims from received claims. Do not combine claims and members. Do not combine Form 19, Form 31, Form 10C, pension and transfer statistics without labelling the combined scope.

### 2. Public recurrence

Find and deduplicate first-person public accounts of:

- blank or missing Form 19 rejection reasons;
- cryptic Form 19 rejection text;
- repeated Form 19 rejection after attempted correction;
- contradictory status across EPFO surfaces;
- a claim marked `Under Process` beyond the official limit;
- rejection visible on one surface but not another;
- claim or tracking identifiers not usable for a specific grievance;
- absence of an evidence-backed correction route.

Report all four date windows separately:

- 22 July 2026 through 21 August 2026;
- 1 July 2026 through 31 July 2026;
- 21 August 2025 through 21 August 2026;
- 1 January 2025 through 31 December 2025.

Use public Reddit, X where accessible, YouTube public material, public finance and consumer forums, public comments, reputable reporting based on named first-person accounts, and other indexable sources. Search English, Hindi and Hinglish variants.

Count unique reviewable public accounts, not mentions and not presumed affected users. Exclude advice-only posts, commercial promotion, copied stories, reposts, second-hand claims, and unverifiable snippets. Keep direct Form 19 evidence separate from Form 31 or unspecified EPFO claims.

If platform coverage is incomplete, report the result as a lower bound. Do not extrapolate it into national prevalence.

### 5. Safe product rule map

Separate:

## Evidence tiers

- Tier A: current official law, scheme, manual, circular, FAQ, annual report, audit, parliamentary document, court record, public-authority page, or official dataset.
- Tier B: peer-reviewed or method-based institutional research.
- Tier C: reputable reporting that opens and correctly represents an underlying source.
- Tier D: first-person public account, proving only that the account was reported.
- Tier E: commercial guide, marketing page, copied article, video advice, or AI summary, useful only as a lead or competitor.

Use Tier A for product rules and official scale. Use Tier D for recurrence only. Do not use Tier E to instruct users.

## Required corrections and stress tests

Test and correct these claims:

1. `One in three Form 19 final-settlement claims are rejected.`
2. `Reason Code 15` has a universal official definition.
3. Every online Form 19 claim requires employer attestation.
4. A missing physical Aadhaar card explains an online claim rejection.
5. Cross-document mismatch can always be detected from user-entered fields.
6. A technical error should be solved by reapplying.
7. EPFiGMS has a 15-to-30-working-day general official timeline.
8. Every `Under Process` claim represents a delay.
9. AI can diagnose the real cause of a blank rejection.
10. No existing tool already provides equivalent guidance.

- the Citizens' Charter seven-working-day aspirational Form 19 target;
