# Frequently asked questions

**Registration**

**Does filling out the registration form reserve my place?**

No. Final participation in the hackathon will be confirmed by email once the initial stage closes.

**Do I need to be a developer?**

No. Developers, designers, product thinkers, students and anyone that wishes to can apply. You should build in teams of up to 4, so you can pick roles for yourself.

**Can I participate alone or as a team?**

You can participate solo or in a team of up to four. The person submitting the registration form is titled the leader and they give names and email addresses of other team members.

---

**Scope of the build**

**What exactly should I build?**

Either a redesign/rebuild of an existing public service site or an entirely new platform that simplifies interactions between residents and administration.

**Is a Figma design enough?**

No. Designs can be part of the submission, but the main journey should be a working prototype.

**Does every feature need to work?**

Every feature you demo must work. If you pretend data is pulled from a live integration that doesn't work, do not include it in the submission.

---

**Technical rules**

**Is Codex mandatory?**

Yes, for the prototype submitted to the hackathon. Codex should be meaningfully involved in the build. You may use other open-source tools and libraries, but your submission should explain how Codex contributed to it. If a public service body chooses to develop the solution further, there are no restrictions on the technology used for that later implementation. The builder and the public service body can decide what best fits the project. If your build is selected among the 10 finalists, OpenAI will provide support.

**Can I use other tools and libraries?**

Yes, provided you have the right to use them and disclose them in your submission.

**Can I use an existing government website as a starting point?**

You can study the existing workflows to figure out how to improve them, but the code / prototype you build should be fresh. If using existing open-source code, you should build meaningfully on top of it.

**Can I connect to a live government system?**

No. Unless widely documented, or specifically approved in official developer documentation, you should not scrape live systems.

**Can I use real user data?**

No. Don't use real personal data, government valid IDs, passwords, OTPs, payment credentials, or other personal information.

**Can I use AI / synthetic data?**

Yes. You can use synthetic data to make the prototype appear realistic and context-quality, but it's better to keep convenient mockups or prototypes.

**Can I submit something I already made?**

You can submit ideas/code earlier if you hold rights to them and disclose it, but your finale presentation should demonstrate that real progress was made within the timeframe of this event.

---

**Screening and judging**

**When is the submission deadline?**

Submit your build by **August 27, 2026.**

**What should the demo video show?**

Record a video that lets reviewers see your final product and walk your user from start to completion. A short walkthrough of the prototype's real flow is ideal. Focus on crispness, no 45 minute video pitches.

**Will visual design alone win?**

Good design matters, but it's not enough. A tight, functional core journey that answers real citizen issues, end-to-end, and works reliably is required.

---

**Finale**

**Will the winning builds be adopted by the government?**

There is no guarantee of adoption. If a public body chooses to adopt or develop a submission, we will help facilitate initial conversations between builders and public agencies.

**Is this an official government hackathon?**

No. It is an independent builder hackathon organized by Varun Mayya in partnership with OpenAI. The aim is to assemble world-class prototypes to inspire and help modernise public services, making them simpler and accessible.

**What happens if I am selected as a finalist?**

You will be invited to Bengaluru to present and celebrate in a finale event. The team will contact you about technical checks, rehearsal, travel, accommodation and filming consent.

**Are there prizes or Codex credits?**

OpenAI will allocate API credits / OpenAI Codex credits for selected finalists for their projects and grant prizes.

---

**IP, data and rights**

**Who will see my registration information and code?**

Registration and submission data will be managed by Varun Mayya's team. If selected, OpenAI team will receive access to this data. The full privacy notice explains how data can be used and can be deleted upon request.

**Who owns my build?**

You do! Any intellectual property you create remains entirely yours. **You retain full rights to the build.**

**Where will updates be shared?**

Through the email address provided in registration form and on relevant public updates channels / updates on community platforms as the event progresses.
